### Websites for Infertility

### Doctors for Fertility

Their goal is to "educate and inform policy on reproductive rights and to advocate for patients to have continued access to fertility treatment and the freedom to build their families"

Has a membership for physicians

### Resolve New England

Large database of support groups, Adoption/IVF/Financial/Lawyers list, and advocacy and educational blog.

"SisterSong Women of Color Reproductive Justice Collective was formed in 1997 by 16 organizations of women of color from four mini-communities (Native American, African American, Latina, and Asian American) who recognized that we have the right and responsibility to represent ourselves and our communities, and the equally compelling need to advance the perspectives and needs of women of color."

### Resolve

The National Infertility Association

Provides significant advocacy, activism, education, and support to the infertility community

### Fertility & Infertility by the CDC

Scientific Research and Data in easy to understand Q&A and flyers on all things related to infertility and reproductive health

### The Lucky Egg

Blog hosted by Dr. Lucky Sekhon, MD an infertility physician trained in reproductive endocrinology working in NY

### Fertility Rally Blog

### Juliet Blog

Fertility Rally has a blog, community groups, virtual confrences and talks, and podcasts talking about everything to do with infertility.

Juliet sells ovulation and pregnancy test, and also has an informative and supportive blog.

"We give $1 of every purchase to organizations that provide reproductive and prenatal care to historically excluded communities, prioritizing organizations that support BIPOC women. "

This website not just has products for patients with pelvic pain, but also has recorded virtual Physical Therapy!
