Welcome! My name is Dr. Moss. and I am a current PGY-3 Psychiatry Chief Resident for Academics (previously MedEd & Wellness).

My goal is to be the liaison of information using my background and expertise to share the ever growing wisdom I have aquired throughout my professional and personal life.

This website contains a narrative medicine Blog, Podcast on humanizing medicine, and an abundant resources continuely aquired during my journey in medical school and residency.

Whether you are a medical student or doctor, I hope I can provide some unique perspectives and meaningful resources!

***All thoughts are my own, not representitive of any organization (includding my employer), and are not medical, psychiatric, nor financial advice***
