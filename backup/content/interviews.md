The episode features an interview with Dr. Marshall, a physician who discusses her unique journey into medicine, specializing in bleeding disorders related to women's health, her personal experience with infertility, navigating IVF, the importance of advocating for diversity and inclusivity in the medical community, and the challenges of balancing parenthood and a medical career.

Meet Vicky a 3rd year medical student who discusses her struggles, failures, and successes. She emphasizes the importance of diversity in medicine, advocates for sharing failures, and encourages self-advocacy.

Dr. Moss interviews Malikiya Hinds, a second-year medical student at Harvard Medical School, originally from Trinidad and Tobago. Malikiya moved to the U.S. in 2016 to pursue her dream of becoming a physician, despite the challenges of being a Black woman with a visible disability. She shares her journey, including the cultural transition, overcoming societal and self-imposed limitations, and the importance of community, mentorship, and representation. Malikiya emphasizes the need to take up space, ask for help, and persist in the face of obstacles. Her story is a testament to resilience, determination, and the power of believing in oneself.

This episode follows the journey of Parul Arora, a nurse practitioner with low vision, as she overcomes challenges and bias to pursue a career in healthcare. She shares her struggles, experiences with disclosure, advocacy work, and the importance of mentorship and support for individuals with disabilities in the medical field.

Dr. Moss interviews Dr. Amruti Borad, DO, an osteopathic, concierge, family medicine physician, and coach. Dr. Borad shares her journey from medical school through various roles in academics and leadership, emphasizing her experience with burnout during the COVID-19 pandemic and how a physician coach helped her recognize the need for a different way to practice medicine. She discusses the importance of mentorship, the differences between coaching, mentorship, and therapy, and the collaborative nature of a coach's role. Dr. Borad also explores her transition to concierge medicine, highlighting the benefit of spending more time with patients and having strong support systems. The discussion touches on the importance of asking for help, setting boundaries, and recognizing when to seek change in one’s career path.

This episode interviews Dr. Elmarie Alexander, a medical doctor from St. Lucia who shares her journey through medical school, residency, and her battle with breast cancer during the COVID-19 pandemic. Dr. Alexander discusses the challenges she faced, including transferring medical schools, navigating residency as a foreign graduate, and coping with her diagnosis and treatment while continuing her education. She emphasizes the importance of empathy, breaking stereotypes, and promoting diversity in the medical field. Dr. Alexander also provides advice for other healthcare professionals with disabilities or health conditions and highlights the significance of community support. The episode aims to inspire and offer hope to those in similar circumstances.

This episode follows the journey of Kimether Redmon, a nurse practitioner, who has had to learn how to navigate working in healthcare with a chronic illness and what lead her down this career path. She talks about her experience doing medical missions, her work in trauma-informed care, and her role in advocacy.

This episode is an interview with Mami, a PhD student with autism, ADHD, and other disabilities, who is set to begin her medical school journey. Mami shares her experiences with her disabilities, how they influenced her path from chemistry research to pursuing a medical degree, and the challenges she faced along the way. She discusses the importance of disclosing disabilities in medical school applications and how her unique strengths have contributed to her academic and personal success. The conversation highlights the need for diversity in the medical field and provides insights into overcoming obstacles while advocating for oneself and others.
