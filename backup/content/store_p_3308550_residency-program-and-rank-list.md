Want to know which Family Medicine and Psychiatry programs accepted my application with "red flags" ( two STEP1 failures and a Leave of Absence), and invited me for an interview?

The first 3 on this list I went back and forth on and last minute switched the program I matched (and is on #1 in this diagram as #3). Always trust your gut!
