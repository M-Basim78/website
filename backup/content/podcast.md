### Podcast 1: Life as a Patient-Doctor

### More Episodes

Dr. Stephanie Moss, MD is the host of the podcast

Life as a Patient Doctor a platform which aims to share diverse and inspiring stories within the medical community.

Through sharing her own lived experience and interviewing other doctors, students, and health care workers, we hope to demonstrate the humanity in medicine.

In this podcast we will delve into crucial topics like Disabilities, Mental Health, Sexual Health, Psychiatry and wellness, and the unique experiences of having a marginalized identity in Medicine.

### Podcast 2: Medical School with Dr. Moss

Medical School with Dr. Moss a platform which aims to uncover the hidden struggles of medical students

Through sharing her own lived experience of failing USMLE Step1, taking a Leave of Absence, and almost being dissmissed for Professionalism, Dr. Moss has a wealth of information to pass on.

In this podcast we will delve into crucial topics like Disabilities, Mental Health, USMLE, Clinical Rotations, Financial Aid and the unique experiences of having a marginalized identity in Medicine.

### Podcast 3: Residency Applications with Dr. Moss

Residency Applications with Dr. Moss a platform which aims provide advice on applying to Residency

Through sharing her own lived experience of dual applying to Residency and matching in her top specialty choice (Psychiatry) in her top 3 choices.

In this podcast we will delve into crucial topics like disability Disclosure, Red Flags, Mock Interviews, Personal Statements, ERAS Applications, & more!
