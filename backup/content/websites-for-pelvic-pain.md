### Websites for Pelvic Pain

### International Society for the Study of Women's Sexual Health

Webinars, research, conferences, advocacy for patients & providers

### International Pelvic Pain Society

Virtual meetings, education, and resources

### National Vulvodynia Association

Education, Research, Support and Advocacy on Vulvovaginal pain

### Vulvar Pain Society

"Resources, practical advice and information for people living with vulval pain, families, healthcare providers and the wider public"

### International Painful Bladder Foundation

"The IPBF is a non-profit, voluntary, umbrella organization, active worldwide, that promotes knowledge and awareness of interstitial cystitis/bladder pain syndrome, Hunner lesion, hypersensitive bladder, chronic pelvic pain and associated disorders among patients, patient support groups, health professionals and the general public. "

### Pelvic Rehabilitation Medicine

Provider of Interdisiciplinary Pelvic Pain Support

### Pelvic Health & Rehabiliation Center

Blog & Videos by Pelvic Physicial Therapists

### Sex, Pain, & Endometriosis

This website is part of a research project funded by the Canadian Institutes of Health Research.

Collaborators include; gynaecologists, allied health professionals and patient partners at the Centre for Pelvic Pain and Endometriosis located at BC Women’s Hospital + Health Centre in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada as well as endometriosis.org and the Endometriosis Network Canada.

### Pelvic Gym

Pelvic Floor PT videos for various pelvic health and pain conditions.

Can do a free trial.

### The Origin

Virtual and in-person Pelvic Floor and Whole Body PT visits for pregnancy, postpartum, sexual health, and menopause.

### Clinici.wiki

A crowd sourcing website which holds resources for health providers and patients. This link is for their chronic pain section.

### Tight Lipped

The Tight Lipped Organization is a group of patients with Pelvic Pain that have come together to advocate for Pelvic pain survivors. Check out their Vinel, Podcast, Webinars, and more!

### Ohnut- Pain Preception Project

Pelvic Pain resources, quiz, and reccomendations on talking with your doctor.
