### STIGMA App (Free)

Instagram: @thestigma.app

We're on a mission to normalize talking about mental health. Step 1: Create an account, it takes less than 2 minutes. Step 2: Engage in the way that feels most comfortable to you. When you're not ready to talk Watch STIGMA Stories to see living proof that you are not alone. If something you watch inspires you to want to talk, click the I want to share my story button. When you're having a rough day Use the Ask for HOPE option to record a video, audio, or text message and share what you're feeling. When you want to make someone's day brighter Whether you're having a good day or a bad one, being kind feels good and is proven to improve your mental health. We encourage you to make Offering HOPE part of your daily mental health routine.

(FREE)

Survivors Speak Series

Survivors Speak is a platform for survivors of childhood trauma and abuse to share their stories, their healing, and the lessons they have learned. There are two ways you can participate and two ways you can witness the stories of others.

1-on-1 Drop In Peer Support & Guidance Sessions($)

Survivor’s Circle Virtual Peer Support Groups ($)

These support groups are facilitated on Zoom giving you as an attendee the ability to turn your camera off and just listen if that is what makes you feel safe.

During these sessions connect with other survivors, hear stories and experiences that resonate, ask questions, and share resources. These groups are a safe space to be seen, heard, and understood because that it what you deserve.
