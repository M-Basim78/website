### Managing Opioid Withdrawal with Suboxone

Monday, January 29, 2024

:

Clinical Case

Patient arrives to the emergency room after receiving 4 mg of Suboxone in an out-patient clinic. Patient is shivering in 10 out of 10 full body pain and holding a plastic bag splattered with vomit. Patient’s pants fall to their ankles as they hobble back and forth to the bathroom from their hospital bed. They scream in desperation that they have diarrhea all over themselves. You notice the patients wet brow as they plead to be given more Suboxone. You are the resident rotating in the ER and have never dosed Suboxone. What do you do?

***This Blog Post on Opioid Withdrawal and Suboxone Dosing is for educational purposes only specifically created for medical students, resident physicians, and licensed medical health providers.***

****This is NOT medical guidance or recommendations. Please follow up with your doctor for specific advice ***

What is precipitated withdrawal?

- When someone is given a “rescue medication” like naloxone and immediately experiences withdrawal symptoms

### Buprenorphine /Naloxone (Suboxone)

- = dissolves

Sublingual film

Half life:

- Bupi 1/2 life is 166 min (2.75 hours) vs fental is 7 min

Why it has naloxone:

- If melt and inject or smoke it then will get more naloxone which will block the opioid receptors leading to instant withdrawal (misusing)

MOA:

- Partial agonist at mu opiod receptor - acute pain relief

- Antagonist at k receptor

- Occupy receptor without fully allowing path

- Higher binding affinities so misplaces opioid in the body

- :

Benefits

- Safer than other opioids

- Reduces co-morbid depression symptoms

- Reduces pain (still on opioid receptor)

- Prevents relapse

- Prevents misuse of other opioids

- Difficult to overdose because has a limit (ceiling affect)

Analogy:

- Stuck key vs other opioid key opens door easy

- is key! Even for patients

Education

- "Suboxone will keep people in treatment" Dr. Christopher

To Note:

- Usually Dose 1x per day

- Timing is important can’t give too close together

- Doesn't cause the "high" but "at right dose patients feel better" Dr. Christopher

Metabolized & Eliminated

- Mostly by feces

- Liver enzyme CYP3A4

Helpful Opioid Conversions:

1mg SL buprenorphine (Suboxone, Subzolv, Subutex) =

0.3mg IV buprenorphine (Buprenex) =

30mg oral morphine equivalence (OME)

Give 2 + 2 + 2 …..

Traditional Ways to Dose:

- https://www.samhsa.gov/medications-substance-use-disorders/statutes-regulations-guidelines

Give 16 + 8 + 8 +….

Getting off of Suboxone:

- Takes time (months)

- Requires rehab and close support

- Increased constipation

- Can increase risk of misuse and overdose

### Other Medications:

Methadone

- schedule 2

- Full opioid agonist

Naloxone:

- IM or Intranasal

- MOA: Full opioid agonist and prevent the opioid in the system from clicking to receptors

- Really quick onset

- May need multiple doses

- ANYONE can pick up Free Intranasal in pharmacies & hospitals

Naltrexone:

- Will activate opioid receptor and help in Alcohol use disorder

- Not used for opioid withdrawl

Fentanyl

- "3rd Waive" of the opioid Crises

- Opioids that are synthetic

- Very hard to get off it

- Holds the opioid receptor very strongly

- Very strong withdrawal symptoms

- Need higher dose of Buprenorphrone (around 32mg in ED)

- Usually require daily methadone clinic

- If combine with Stimulant (Methaphentamine) then have a higher risk for death because competing receptors that increase and decrease respiratory rate

Poly-substance use:

Xylazine or Tranq:

- MOA: a2 agonist with affects of central and peripheral nervous system

- Powerful muscle relaxant

- When you see individuals bent over while standing

- Very potent

- Mixed with other drugs

- Interesting Video on a city of people on Tranq:

Overdoses and deaths:

- When people return to their original dose after they have been off of it (rehab, hospital)

Importance of Motivational Interviewing:

- There is always 1 reason to stop such as:

- Able to improve relationship with family or friends

- Able to get back to work

- Able to have a stable home life

- Activate Values

- “I am wondering about your deceptive tendencies?”
