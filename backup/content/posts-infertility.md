### MedPsycMoss' Posts on Living with Infertility

### Why Talk About Infertility?

I found out I was going to have trouble reproducing before I was even remotely thinking about becoming pregnant. I was a second year medical student studdying Sex & Reproduction when I came upon a medical condition that sounded exactly like everything I had experienced for most of my teenage and adult life. A couple months later I was finanlly diagnosed with endometriosis through surgery. However, more importantly, I learned my endometrial tubes were clogged = infertility.

Here is my journey through writings, blog posts, social media posts, and talks.
