### Sign up for Personalized Advising with Dr. Moss

Are you navigating the challenging journey of college or medical school? Meet Dr.Moss (@medpsycmoss) an incoming Psychiatry resident and a trailblazer in the medical community. As a first-generation medical student who persevered through obstacles, including a stumble with STEP1 and a leave of absence, Dr. Moss understands the unique challenges you may face!

Specialized Support:

Strategize your medical school or residency applications, navigate the intricacies of medical education as a low-income, first-generation student, and harness effective study strategies. With insights into mental health and wellness, Dr. Moss is here to guide you towards success.

Compassionate Guidance:

Living with invisible disabilities herself, Dr. Moss fosters an inclusive environment where every individual's unique challenges are understood and supported. Through compassionate coaching, Dr. Moss will help you overcome obstacles and achieve your dreams in the medical field.
