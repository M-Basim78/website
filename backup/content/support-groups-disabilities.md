### Support Groups for living with Disabilities

[Disability Lead](https://www.disabilitylead.org/about)

Their Members and Fellows lead with their disability identity to create better, equitable and more inclusive communities. Network and connect with disabilitity leaders.

Apply for a 1 year fellowship training!

[Docs with Disabilities](https://www.docswithdisabilities.org/)

Support group of medical providers and students with disabilities. Has an amazing podcast!

Book Clubs, Symposiums, Workshops

On Twitter:

[@DocsWith](https://twitter.com/DocsWith)

### Doctors for Fertility

Their goal is to "educate and inform policy on reproductive rights and to advocate for patients to have continued access to fertility treatment and the freedom to build their families"

Has a membership for physicians

Organization that works to promote equity for people with disabilities and chronic health conditions in nursing through education and advocacy.
