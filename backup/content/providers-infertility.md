### 3. Interview Providers

What is your specific training and qualifications?

How long have you been practicing?

How often would you anticipate seeing me? For how long?

What types of goals do make? What is "success" for you in my situation?

What is a typical session like? How long are each sessions?

Who am I working with each time I come to the office? A doctor, nurse, therapist?

What kind of homework/reading do you give patients?

Have you been in therapy yourself? Do you still? (for therapist)

How much do you charge? What are you sliding-scale options? Do you offer financial councling?

How many clients have you had with similar circumstances to my own?

How comfortable do you feel about working with ______(special population or circumstance)?

What are the ways I can contact you on an emergency & non-emergency situation?

Are you affiliated with any academic or research institutions?
