### The Unchosen Path: Navigating Medical Challenges Beyond Our Control

Friday, March 18, 2022

I chose to go into medicine

I chose to move out to Chicago to start medical school

I chose to get an explorative surgery to see if i had endometriosis

I did not choose to have find that my urine tubes were clogged and that I had endometriosis

I did not choose for my ovaries to have a limiting reserve of eggs

I did not choose to have infertility

So where is this choice you speak of?

Yes I chose to take charge of my health

I chose to be an advocate and speak up when I could no longer tolerate my pain

I chose to increase the chance that I can have a child in the future by removing my eggs while I still can

But I didn't choose to inject myself 2 times a day for over 2 weeks

I didn’t choose to be bent over and crying in pain after every injecton, car ride, or work shift

I didn’t choose to get hyperstimulated ovarian syndrome from overworking my ovaries to produce more eggs

But you are right I choose to go through with follicle removal at your hospital

My insurance had confirmed with me multiple times that the infertility procedure was going to be fully covered

But obviously that was not the case because now I have bill almost twice my monthly rent

The out of pocket max is four times my rent so there is no way I could even cover that

You said financial assistance was there to help those in need who cannot pay their medical bills

Never did I see anything about your office dictating what procedures are worthy or unworthy of receiving financial assistance

You said I have a choice?

No one chooses to have a medical condition
