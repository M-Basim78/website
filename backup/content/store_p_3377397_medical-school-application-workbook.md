### Medical School Application Workbook

### $ 10.00

Introducing the Medical School Application Workbook by Dr. Stephanie Moss, MD – your essential companion for the application cylcle.

Dr. Moss successfully applied to both MD & DO schools during the 2018-2019 cycle, securing over 10 interviews and ultimately being accepted into her 5 medical schools. With a focus on maximizing opportunities and navigating the complexities of residency applications, she crafted this workbook to share her proven strategies with you.

Inside, you'll find pages meticulously designed to guide you through every step of your medical school application process. From finding LOR writers, building a exstrodinary personal statement, deciding which activities are meaningful, determining what is important to you in a medical school, and how to balence applying to multiple programs.

40+ worksheet-style

These resources are built on Dr. Moss's personal experiences and years of advising pre-med and medical students.

Key features include:

- Step-by-step guidance for crafting a standout personal statement

- Strategies for choosing the right medical school

- Checklist for Dual Applying (MD & DO)

- Tips for securing interviews and making a lasting impression

- Insights on balancing personal and professional goals

Plus, enjoy unlimited access to updates via email, ensuring you always have the most current advice and resources at your fingertips.

Take charge of your medschool journey and achieve your dreams with confidence. Get your Medical School Application Workbook today!
