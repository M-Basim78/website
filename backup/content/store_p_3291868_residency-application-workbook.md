### Residency Application Workbook

### $ 12.00

Introducing the Residency Application Workbook by Dr. Stephanie Moss, MD – your essential companion for the 2025-2026 ERAS cycle!

Dr. Moss successfully applied to both Psychiatry and Family Medicine during the 2023-2024 cycle, securing over 24 interviews and ultimately matching into her top-choice specialty and program. With a focus on maximizing opportunities and navigating the complexities of residency applications, she crafted this workbook to share her proven strategies with you.

Inside, you'll find pages meticulously designed to guide you through every step of your residency application process. From finding LOR writers, building a exstrodinary personal statement, deciding which activities are meaningful, determining what is important to you in a residency program, and how to balence applying to multiple programs. These resources are built on Dr. Moss's personal experiences and years of advising pre-med and medical students.

45+ worksheet-style

Key features include:

- Step-by-step guidance for crafting a standout personal statement

- Strategies for choosing the right specialties and programs

- Checklist for Dual Applying

- Tips for securing interviews and making a lasting impression

- Insights on balancing personal and professional goals

Plus, enjoy unlimited access to updates via email, ensuring you always have the most current advice and resources at your fingertips.

Take charge of your residency journey and achieve your dreams with confidence. Get your Residency Application Workbook today!
