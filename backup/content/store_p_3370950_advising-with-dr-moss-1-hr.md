### Advising with Dr. Moss - 1 hr

### $ 70.00

Are you navigating the challenging journey of premed, college, medical school, or residency?

Dr.Moss is a PGY2 Chief Psychiatry Resident and a trailblazer in the medical community. As a first-generation medical student who persevered through obstacles, including two failures with STEP1, health conditions, and a leave of absence, Dr. Moss understands the unique challenges and struggles you may face!

Empowerment Through Lived Experience: Dr. Moss and her husband also Dr. Moss, a PGY3 Neurology resident, embarked on their medical journey together from senior year of high school. Together they overcome countless hurdles and celebrated victories together.

Drawing from her lived experience, Dr. Moss offers personalized advising to help you thrive academically, mentally, and professionally.

She has advised and coached individuals with the following challenges:

Failing exams, courses, MCAT/USMLE/COMLEX

Struggling applying for disability accommodations

Working through possible or REAL dissmissal from programs

Debating or Working Through a Leave of Absense (or gap years)

Pivoting career paths in and outside of medicine

Premeds debating if this is this right path for them

Applying with "red flags" or as an IMG

& so much more!

Specialized Support:

Strategize your medical school or residency navigate the intricacies of medical education with mental/physicial health conditions, or learn how to harness effective study strategies.With insights into mental health and wellness, Dr. Moss is here to guide you towards success! What ever that means for YOU

applications,

Compassionate Life/Education/Career Coaching:

Living with invisible disabilities herself, Dr. Moss fosters an inclusive environment where every individual's unique challenges are understood and supported. Through personalized advising, Dr. Moss will help you overcome obstacles and achieve your dreams (what ever they may be).

Take the first step towards realizing your aspirations. Schedule a 30min or 60min coaching session with Dr. Moss and embark on a transformative experience tailored to your individual needs.
