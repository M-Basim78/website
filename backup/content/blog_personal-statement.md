### Medical School Personal Statement: A Journey from the Dojo to Medicine

Friday, September 08, 2023

“Attention!” “Yes, ma’am!”

My students snapped their legs together, their sweaty palms glued to the sides of their legs.

“Bow...Begin!”

My eyes scanned the floor, ready to dart to a student to adjust their loose fists or bent legs. I strove for my students to achieve perfection, and envisioned each of them as already having completed their goal of black belt. Walking upon that sweat-stained mat, I felt complete. I loved the responsibility, control, and power I had earned by being one of the highest ranked black belts in my Tae Kwon Do studio.

However, the studio was not where I saw myself long-term.

During the summer after graduating from high school, I began to explore careers in the medical field. Through certifying as an Advanced-Emergency Medical Technician (A-EMT), I improved my ability to problem solve and take control of any situation at hand. By analyzing my patients critically, I was able to quickly decide what actions to execute during the emergency situation.
