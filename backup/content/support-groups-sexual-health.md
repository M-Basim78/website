Survivors Speak Series

Survivors Speak is a platform for survivors of childhood trauma and abuse to share their stories, their healing, and the lessons they have learned. There are two ways you can participate and two ways you can witness the stories of others.

1-on-1 Drop In Peer Support & Guidance Sessions($)

Survivor’s Circle Virtual Peer Support Groups ($)

These support groups are facilitated on Zoom giving you as an attendee the ability to turn your camera off and just listen if that is what makes you feel safe.

During these sessions connect with other survivors, hear stories and experiences that resonate, ask questions, and share resources. These groups are a safe space to be seen, heard, and understood because that it what you deserve.
