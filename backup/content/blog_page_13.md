Many of these topics involve medicine, mental and physical health. Through narative medicine I hope that these stories provide a greater understanding of what individuals go through. In addition to writing on my own journey and the anonymous (de-identified) stories of patients, I have included research and resources. In turn, I hope to provide insight and power to disempowered voices.
