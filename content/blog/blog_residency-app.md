What is my purpose in medicine?

"I can’t quite say it is one thing but it is an accumulation: To medically take care of others. To take care of people as people that includes their mental state, their life, and physical body. I know it is a bit ambitious but I know it can be done even if it is just for a couple people." 6/2021

"I have found my voice and I have the power to share my ideas, experiences, and lessons I have learned with others.” 5/2021

“That is what I can advocate for, no matter who the person is all they want in life is to be seen and heard.“ 12/2019

“The practice of viewing health from a psychological and trauma informed lens is called integrative medicine. This is why I am pursuing my medical degree, so I can help others look back at the various threats they have and are currently encountering and see how they are creating negative impacts on their health. Through looking for the 'root causes' one can pursue better informed health care decisions and management.”

Give 1 Key Patient Encounter:

Sickle cell child - Cried for mother but was more myself and realizing that he was so lucky to have a mother caring for him

Patient with Delirium - Calming down a delirious screaming patient through holding their hands and talking in a low, calm voice and asking about their work/pets/hobbies & suggesting to our team we get music & dog therapy

Medicare - Helping a family get on medicare after learning that their medications & hospital stay will be too expensive (they qualified for support) - after learning that the hospital case management is not allowed to help them get on insurance per “hospital policy”

Social Determinates - Asking about living environment & knowing what it meant when they said they couldn’t afford to pay the electrical bill so it was off & at discharge this they would be in 100F weather. I worked with them to apply for electrical financial support through the city

Family - Called a patient’s family member to update them & let them know the time for them to be in the patient’s room so they can ask all the questions to the doctor when we visit the patient on morning physician rounds
