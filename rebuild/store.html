Before you buy

How delivery actually works

WORKBOOKS

Digital workbooks are delivered after purchase. The Testing Accommodations Workbook is too large to download, so she emails it within 12 to 24 hours. If it has not arrived, email medpsycmoss@gmail.com.

SESSIONS

When you buy a mock interview you get the link to her calendar and book the slot that suits you. The session is 30 minutes plus feedback, and you choose whether it is recorded so you can watch yourself back.

EDITING

Editing is one pass with a 48 to 72 hour turnaround, covering content, voice, tone and proofreading. Add a dual application with two personal statements, or two edits of the same statement, at checkout.
