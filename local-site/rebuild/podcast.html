SHOW 01 · INTERVIEWS

Life as a Patient-Doctor

Dr. Stephanie Moss hosts a platform that aims to share diverse and inspiring stories within the medical community. Through her own lived experience and interviews with other doctors, students and health care workers, the show sets out to demonstrate the humanity in medicine.

Episodes go into disabilities, mental health, sexual health, psychiatry and wellness, and the unique experience of holding a marginalized identity in medicine.

Spotify Apple Podcasts YouTube Amazon Music
SHOW 02 · MED SCHOOL

Medical School with Dr. Moss

A platform that aims to uncover the hidden struggles of medical students. Dr. Moss draws on her own experience of failing USMLE Step 1, taking a leave of absence and almost being dismissed for professionalism.

Episodes go into disabilities, mental health, the USMLE, clinical rotations, financial aid and the unique experience of holding a marginalized identity in medicine.

Spotify Apple Podcasts YouTube
SHOW 03 · RESIDENCY

Residency Applications with Dr. Moss

A platform that aims to give advice on applying to residency. Dr. Moss dual applied and matched in her top specialty choice, psychiatry, within her top three choices.

Episodes go into disability disclosure, red flags, mock interviews, personal statements, ERAS applications and more.
