I value people with disabilities through living with my brother-in law and while working at Imagine as a case manager. I value working with older patients and those close to death, who rarely have anyone to advocate for them. I also want to work with kids who have been abused to help show them they can make it” “I want to help people who are disempowered, disenfranchised, and not usually given the same amount of resources and focus and care as others. This includes Hispanics and blacks, LGBTQ+, trauma patients, females, and homeless populations. I want to address their whole body, mind, and soul. I want to help people who have had trauma. This includes helping them get social work and support for food, living, healthcare and all the basic needs they deserve. “ 4/2021 What is my purpose in medicine? "I can’t quite say it is one thing but it is an accumulation: To medically take care of others. To take care of people as people that includes their mental state, their life, and physical body. I know it is a bit ambitious but I know it can be done even if it is just for a couple people." 6/2021 "I have found my voice and I have the power to share my ideas, experiences, and lessons I have learned with others.” 5/2021 “That is what I can advocate for, no matter who the person is all they want in life is to be seen and heard.“ 12/2019 “The practice of viewing health from a psychological and trauma informed lens is called integrative medicine. This is why I am pursuing my medical degree, so I can help others look back at the various threats they have and are currently encountering and see how they are creating negative impacts on their health.
