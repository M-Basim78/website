Endometriosis.org is the global platform which links all stake holders in endometriosis - one of the most common causes of pelvic pain and infertility in women.

Sex, Pain, & Endometriosis

This website is part of a research project funded by the Canadian Institutes of Health Research.

Collaborators include; gynaecologists, allied health professionals and patient partners at the Centre for Pelvic Pain and Endometriosis located at BC Women’s Hospital + Health Centre in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada as well as endometriosis.org and the Endometriosis Network Canada.

One of the major advocates of Endometriosis and strives to raise money for research on this chronic illness.

This website not just has products for patients with pelvic pain, but also has recorded virtual Physical Therapy!
